# CardArchived — Working Business Plan

## Executive Summary

CardArchived is a permanent digital identity, research, presentation and provenance layer for collectible objects. The system assigns a permanent archive identifier to a specific physical object and builds a structured record around it: object metadata, preserved photography, specimen fingerprint, verification workflow, artistic presentation, creator attribution, subject/story/notables, provenance, market history and research sources.

CardArchived is not intended to replace third-party condition grading. It adds information that conventional grading largely does not capture: the identity of the individual specimen, its visual/artistic significance, its evolving provenance and market history, and a permanent public record reachable through a stable URL/QR architecture.

## Core Customer Promise

**Preserve the object. Map the specimen. Tell the story. Make the record portable.**

A collector should be able to encounter a CardArchived object years later and retrieve the same permanent identity record, while separately viewing later research, provenance and market updates.

## Initial Product — $29.99 Archive Registration

Working retail price: **$29.99 per submitted object**.

The initial service is digital-first. It should produce a permanent archive ID and structured object profile using the canonical CardArchived process. The exact included scope must be standardized before commercial launch so that a $29.99 submission does not accidentally promise unlimited manual research.

Core record architecture may include:
- permanent SL-###### archive ID and URL/QR target;
- object record and submitted photography;
- image-derived specimen fingerprint where evidence permits;
- blind same-object verification framework;
- Artistic Presentation Art/Subscript classification;
- creator attribution status, including Attribution Under Research where unresolved;
- Story / Art / Subject / Notables / Why This Object Matters;
- provenance and market layers capable of later updates;
- research/source record and version history.

## Artistic Presentation

CardArchived Artistic Presentation is separate from condition grading and market value.

Internal numerical methodology is not publicly displayed:
- 1–4 = Below Common
- 5–8 = Standard
- 9 = Exceptional
- 10 = Exceptional + Gold Plate eligibility

The public record displays only the Art/Subscript classification. Gold Plate status cannot be purchased or upgraded through a fee.

## Verification Products

### Verify This Card
Compares new photography against a previously frozen specimen fingerprint to answer a narrow question: whether the presented object is consistent with the physical specimen already archived under that ID. Holder identity, certification number and grade are excluded as fingerprint evidence.

### Verify Authenticity
A separate evidence track. Image analysis can organize evidence and contradictions but must not be represented as microscopic, chemical, dimensional or hands-on authentication unless those methods were actually performed.

## Customer Segments

Collectors; dealers and card shops; auction/consignment businesses; collection managers; estates; artists/creators; manufacturers and grading companies; museums/archive-minded collectors; marketplaces and platforms seeking durable object identity or provenance infrastructure.

## Revenue Architecture

### Phase 1 — Direct archive registration
$29.99 base registration creates the installed base and permanent object graph.

### Phase 2 — Additive services
Potential future revenue layers include verification events, enhanced research, provenance documentation, market-history services, premium physical protectors/displays, QR labels, archival presentation products and institutional/dealer tools. These should be priced separately rather than silently expanding the $29.99 workload.

### Phase 3 — B2B / infrastructure
Potential licensing/API/integration relationships with dealers, auction houses, marketplaces, grading companies, manufacturers and collection-management platforms. The permanent archive identifier becomes more valuable as it is referenced across transactions and systems.

## Operating Model

The process must be template-driven and auditable. Source evidence is preserved; machine-readable record data is separate from public presentation; fingerprint evidence is frozen before blind comparison; later research is versioned; unresolved attribution remains unresolved; permanent IDs are never recycled.

Human review should concentrate on judgment-heavy areas: fingerprint selection/lock, artistic classification, contradictory evidence, attribution, sensitive provenance claims and publication approval. Repetitive formatting, indexing and page generation should be automated.

## Competitive Position

CardArchived should avoid positioning itself as merely another grader. Its differentiated product is the combination of:

**specimen identity + artistic presentation + permanent archive + story/research + provenance + verification + market context.**

The long-term defensibility is the accumulated object graph, consistent methodology, trusted historical records and integrations—not simply a webpage or QR code.

## Launch Sequence

1. Finish SL-000001 through SL-000003 under one canonical methodology.
2. Process SL-000004 entirely through the new workflow and measure labor/time.
3. Build the public archive template and permanent routing.
4. Run blind same-object/different-object tests and preserve results.
5. Establish submission terms, evidence disclaimers, privacy/publication controls and correction/version policy.
6. Pilot a small paid cohort at $29.99.
7. Measure unit economics and failure modes before increasing volume.
8. Add market/provenance and B2B integrations only after the core archive process is stable.

## Key Metrics

Paid registrations; completion time per object; direct variable cost per object; gross margin per registration; percentage requiring manual escalation; verification false-positive/false-negative test results; repeat collectors; records receiving provenance updates; QR/profile visits; dealer adoption; support incidents; correction rate; creator-attribution verification rate.

## Strategic Principle

Do not scale submissions faster than evidence quality and operational consistency can scale. A permanent archive accumulates trust slowly and can lose it quickly. The first objective is not maximum volume; it is a process that still deserves to be trusted at volume.
