## Blind Comparison Protocol

1. Ignore holder company, grade, certification, slab insert, slab scratches and holder damage as specimen fingerprint evidence.
2. Evaluate the physical card evidence against frozen M01–M10.
3. Return MATCH, POSSIBLE MATCH, MISMATCH, or NOT EVALUABLE for each feature.
4. Do not invent or redefine a feature after viewing an unknown specimen.
5. Negative evidence matters: a clearly absent frozen anomaly may contradict identity.
6. Shared printed design alone is insufficient to establish specimen identity.
7. State photography limitations including perspective, glare, focus, compression and slab plastic.
8. Summarize matched, possible, mismatched and not-evaluable features, strongest positive and contradictory evidence, geometric consistency, evidence quality, identity confidence and final verdict.
