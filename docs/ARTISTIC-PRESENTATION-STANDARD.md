# CardArchived Artistic Presentation Standard

## Purpose

CardArchived evaluates the collectible as a visual/artistic object separately from condition grading, specimen identity, rarity, market value, celebrity, ownership, or investment potential.

## Internal Evaluation Scale — Confidential Methodology

The numerical score is an internal classification mechanism. It is not displayed on the public object record.

- **1–4 → Below Common**
- **5–8 → Standard**
- **9 → Exceptional**
- **10 → Exceptional + Gold Plate designation**

A public record displays the Art/Subscript classification, not the numerical score.

## Public Art/Subscript Classifications

### Below Common
The object's visual presentation falls below ordinary expectations for its collectible category. The archive must explain the visual/design deficiencies and any successful elements. Critique the object, not the creator.

### Standard
The broad professional range. This includes competent, strong, and even highly accomplished collectible design. An excellent object can remain Standard. The purpose is to prevent classification inflation.

### Exceptional
Reserved for objects that materially separate themselves from excellent conventional production through defensible artistic characteristics such as composition, imagery, typography, color, material integration, conceptual strength, dimensionality, interaction with light, narrative coherence, originality, or display character.

Exceptional is intentionally rare.

## Gold Plate

The Gold Plate is **not a separate public classification**. It is the eventual physical/digital presentation distinction for an object receiving an internal artistic evaluation of 10.

A 9 is publicly **Exceptional**.  
A 10 is publicly **Exceptional** and qualifies for the **Gold Plate**.

Gold Plate status cannot be purchased, requested, upgraded through a fee, or awarded because of market value, rarity, condition, celebrity, or ownership. It can result only from the Artistic Presentation assessment.

## Evaluation Discipline

Before an internal 9 is assigned, the evaluator must answer:

> What does this object accomplish artistically that an excellent Standard object does not?

Before an internal 10 is assigned, the evaluator must also answer:

> What makes this object exceptional even among objects already worthy of Exceptional classification?

If the relevant question cannot be answered convincingly from observable and researched evidence, the threshold has not been met.

## Public Presentation

Public records should show:

**ARTISTIC PRESENTATION**  
**Classification: BELOW COMMON / STANDARD / EXCEPTIONAL**

followed by a written analysis of the applicable visual dimensions. No public numerical score is displayed.

## Relationship to Third-Party Grades

CardArchived Artistic Presentation does not replace or reinterpret PSA, SGC, BGS, CGC or other condition grades. Third-party condition grade and CardArchived Art/Subscript classification are separate information layers.
